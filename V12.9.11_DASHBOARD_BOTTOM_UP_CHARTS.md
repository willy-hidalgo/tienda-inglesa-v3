# v12.9.11 — Dashboard bottom-up operational charts

Dashboard-only change. The productive model and official wMAPE definition are unchanged.

- Section, store and SKU chart forecasts are materialized as the daily sum of final SKU+Store forecasts.
- Actuals for those levels are materialized from the same leaves.
- Store/section direct RLS forecasts remain in `forecast.parquet` for modeling/diagnostics but are no longer the main operational chart series.
- SKU+Store charts remain the productive leaf series.
- `metrics.parquet` is unchanged: official wMAPE remains the existing leaf-level bottom-up calculation with y != 0.
- Optional dashboard expander shows daily leaf-level absolute-error contribution, i.e. the exact numerator contribution of the existing bottom-up wMAPE.
